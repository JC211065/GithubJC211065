About This Website

JC Streetwear Clothes Designing

This website is about designing your pants, shirts, and hoodies exactly how you like them.

Me

My name is Jose Coral. I was born in Colombia and moved to the United States when I was 10. I currently attend Lynn Classical High School. One of my favorite hobbies is playing soccer. My goal is to create a website where people can design their own clothes in their favorite colors and styles.

Why I Made This

I created this website to help people express themselves through fashion. Everyone has their own style, and I want to help people feel confident wearing clothes they design themselves.

Future Plans

In the future, I plan to expand this website to include clothing for all ages and sizes, and possibly even clothing for pets. I want to continue adding more customizable options.
